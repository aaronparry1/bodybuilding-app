# Representative hypertrophy Lower programme-guidance policy

The sole supported Lower input matches the intermediate four-day Upper/Lower hypertrophy family and uses `Lower` / `lower-a` with `full_gym`. It emits: primary knee dominant (3–4), primary hip hinge (2–3), secondary unilateral knee dominant (2–3), knee flexion (2–3), calves (2–3), and optional trunk stability (2–3).

This is 13–19 direct session sets. Knee-dominant and hinge jobs provide overlapping quad/glute/hamstring stimulus without replacing direct knee-flexion or plantar-flexion jobs. A separate glute accessory is intentionally omitted to avoid redundant fatigue. No exercise, ID, exact target or runtime caller exists.
