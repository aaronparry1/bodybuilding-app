# Current hypertrophy-calibration programme policy

`intermediate_upper_lower_hypertrophy_calibration_v1` is the dedicated, pre-exercise policy for the exact intermediate, four-day, full-gym Upper/Lower family in `hypertrophy_calibration` with a normal productive microcycle. It is distinct from base productive hypertrophy and emits training jobs only: no exercises, stable slot IDs, exact targets, plan state or readiness decision.

## Calibration objective

The policy creates repeatable opportunities to establish exercise fit, initial-load and set-tolerance evidence, set-to-set drop-off, shutdown facts, planned-role completion and exact-target evidence breadth. It deliberately does not maximise productive volume, choose a successor, implement load calibration, or evaluate readiness.

## Four-session structure and guidance

| Template | Ordered jobs | Direct guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper-A | horizontal press; horizontal pull; vertical press; vertical pull; lateral deltoid | 7–12 |
| Lower-A | knee dominant; hip hinge; knee flexion; calves | 6–10 |
| Upper-B | vertical pull; vertical press; horizontal pull; horizontal press; lateral deltoid | 7–12 |
| Lower-B | hip hinge; knee-dominant complement; knee flexion; calves | 6–10 |

The four-session total is **26–44**, below base policy's **58–84**. Each policy slot is required and uses `never_omit`; direct arms, trunk stability, Lower-A unilateral quadriceps and Lower-B quadriceps isolation are deterministically absent.

Lower-B's knee-dominant complement is 2–3 sets. D2.5D3A reconciled an earlier arithmetic contradiction: 1–2 would have yielded 5–9 for Lower-B and 25–43 overall. The increase preserves complementary quadriceps evidence without adding a hinge, glute accessory or any new slot.

## Stability and substitution

Every slot preserves target and movement pattern. The metadata favours stable, repeatable exercise selection; later selection/intervention code must not rotate for novelty or merely because immediate progression is absent. This policy does not select, retain or substitute an exercise itself.

## Certification and mapping

`hypertrophy_calibration_upper_lower_certification_v1` certifies exact role order, required movement coverage, A/B priority differentiation, 26–44 guidance, omitted accessory density, stable substitution metadata and the isolated D2/D1 construction proof. The exact registry row now supports slot resolution and D2 construction only. Persistence and current-guidance adjustment remain disabled; no plan-creation or runtime caller consumes the registry.
