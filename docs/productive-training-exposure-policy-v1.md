# Productive Training Exposure Policy v1

Decision ID: 10E

Status: Locked architecture.

## Purpose

The Productive Training Exposure Policy determines whether further work in the current exercise or session is still expected to produce meaningful adaptation.

The objective is not to complete the maximum amount of work. The objective is to complete the minimum productive work required to achieve today's training effect.

## Scope

This policy governs only the active workout.

It does not permanently change future programming. Permanent learning belongs to the Coaching Evidence Engine.

## Training Effect Remaining

- high
- moderate
- low
- negligible
- negative

## Productive Exposure Actions

- continue training
- complete objective
- reduce exposure
- terminate exercise
- terminate session

## Decision Hierarchy

1. Safety
2. Pain
3. Mission objective achieved
4. Diminishing training effect
5. Recovery budget exhaustion
6. Time constraints

## Rules

Safety always overrides productivity.

Pain may terminate immediately.

Once the primary objective has been achieved, unnecessary additional work should be avoided.

Lower-priority session layers are removed before mission-critical work.

Repeated poor-quality work reduces exposure.

AMRAP and fatigue-based methods must respect effort caps.

Backoff work is removed before primary work is compromised.

Mission-critical work is protected wherever safely possible.

Junk volume is not rewarded.

Training effect is prioritised ahead of workout completion percentage.

## Outputs

- training effect remaining
- productive exposure status
- recommended action
- affected session layers
- reason codes
- safety flags
- evidence flags for 9J

## Boundaries

This policy evaluates whether continuing the current exercise or session is productive.

It does not:

- modify future programming
- update the athlete model
- choose exercises
- choose methods
- prescribe future progression
- replace the Coaching Evidence Engine

## Regression Protections

Tests must prove:

- safety overrides productivity
- mission completion is recognised
- junk volume is prevented
- lower-priority layers are removed first
- training effect beats workout completion percentage
- permanent learning remains within 9J
