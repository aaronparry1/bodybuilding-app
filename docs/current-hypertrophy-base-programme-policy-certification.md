# Hypertrophy base programme-policy certification

`hypertrophy_base` is catalogue-defined as stable, recoverable progressive muscle-specific work after calibration, with progression evidence and successors to volume, specialisation or consolidation. The exact intermediate four-day full-gym Upper/Lower policy is certified equivalent at `hypertrophy_base_upper_lower_equivalence_v1`.

The trace locks four A/B roles, full press/pull and lower movement coverage, primary/secondary/isolation purposes, 16–23/13–19/16–23/13–19 direct guidance, deterministic arms/trunk optionality, preserve-target substitution constraints, normal productive priority, planned-role/exact-target evidence compatibility, and successor/deload neutrality. Any mismatch rejects certification.

The mapping registry now marks only the exact base family `supported`, enabling slot resolution and D2 programme construction. It still forbids persistence and guidance application. Calibration, volume, specialisation, consolidation and transition remain dedicated-policy-required. D2.5D3 is the next calibration-policy phase; production new-plan D3 remains blocked because new plans start in calibration.
