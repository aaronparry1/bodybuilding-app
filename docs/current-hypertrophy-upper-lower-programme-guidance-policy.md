# Hypertrophy Upper/Lower A/B programme-guidance policy

Upper-B preserves all four push/pull movement families but shifts vertical pull then vertical press to first priority. Upper-A stays horizontal press/pull first. Lower-B shifts hip hinge first, retains knee-dominant coverage, changes the complementary quadriceps job to lower-fatigue isolation, and retains knee flexion, calves and optional trunk.

Across A/B, all major upper push/pull patterns and lower knee/hip/knee-flexion/calf jobs remain covered. The policies are pre-exercise only; all template and slot IDs are supplied later by D2. Unsupported policies remain explicit.
