# Iron Logic Copy Style Guide

Date: 2026-06-06

Iron Logic should sound like a sharp coach with receipts.

Not a database.
Not a fake scientist.
Not a wellness app in beige trousers.

## Voice

Iron Logic copy should be:

- Direct.
- Coach-like.
- Punchy.
- Clear.
- Confident.
- Slightly brutal when useful.
- Funny without becoming unserious.
- Grounded in logged performance.

The app should feel like:

**A coach that happens to have a training system underneath.**

Not:

**A training system explaining itself.**

## Core Copy Rule

Default screen copy answers:

1. What do I do now?
2. Why?
3. What happens if I act?

Detailed logic belongs behind:

- Why?
- Evidence
- Details
- Advanced

## Copy Principles

### 1. Be Specific

Bad:

“Training is improving.”

Better:

“Your productive sets are holding and shutdowns are low.”

Even better:

“Training is moving. Stay the course.”

Why:

The first sentence is evidence. The second sentence is the decision.

### 2. Lead With The Decision

Bad:

“Based on recent fatigue and performance trends, workload modification may be beneficial.”

Better:

“Reduce workload before pushing harder.”

Why:

Users are in the gym. Give them the move first.

### 3. Keep Evidence Behind The Curtain Until Asked

Visible:

“Try 105kg next set.”

Why:

“You hit the top of the range for 3 productive sets at 100kg.”

### 4. Make Holding Feel Productive

Bad:

“No progression.”

Better:

“Hold the load. More clean reps first.”

Why:

Holding is not failure. It is often exactly the right move.

### 5. Make Stopping Feel Like Coaching

Bad:

“Failed threshold.”

Better:

“Move on. The useful work is done.”

Why:

Shutdown should feel like the coach saved the session, not punished the user.

### 6. Be Honest About Uncertainty

Bad:

“Recommended load: 80kg.”

Better:

“Estimated 80kg from similar lifts. Adjust during warm-ups.”

Why:

Confidence builds trust. Fake certainty spends it.

### 7. Make Low-History States Useful

Bad:

“No data available.”

Better:

“Log 3-5 workouts first. Then the coach can start making real calls.”

Sharper:

“Log a few sessions first. The app is smart, not psychic.”

## Surface Guidance

### Home

Home should sound like a daily briefing.

Use:

- “Today: Push.”
- “Finish the session you started.”
- “Rest today. Next up: Lower.”
- “Week complete. Move the programme forward when you are ready.”
- “Log a few sessions first. The app is smart, not psychic.”

Avoid:

- “Current block metadata.”
- “Performance-based dose.”
- “Strategic signal.”
- “Generated workout.”

### Train Overview

Train should sound practical and fast.

Use:

- “Warm-up”
- “Work sets”
- “Performance”
- “Logged · tap to edit”
- “Add exercise”
- “Cancel workout”
- “Complete workout”

Avoid:

- “Workout exercise metadata”
- “Quality set threshold”
- “Autoregulation engine”
- “Session instance”

### Performance Drawer

The drawer should be blunt.

Use:

- “Load”
- “Reps”
- “Target: 8-12 reps”
- “Suggested: 100kg”
- “Log set”

Avoid:

- “Submit performance”
- “Prescribed performance value”
- “Record training datum”

### Recommendations

Use one clear action.

Increase:

- “You earned more weight.”
- “Try 105kg next set.”

Hold:

- “Hold the weight.”
- “Earn cleaner reps first.”

Reduce:

- “Back it down.”
- “Recent output says this load is too expensive.”

Deload:

- “Pull back before pushing again.”
- “Fatigue is winning right now.”

Rotation:

- “This lift has stopped paying rent.”
- “Swap it, or keep it if setup still feels good.”

### Session Prep

Prep should feel optional and athletic.

Use:

- “Prime the session.”
- “Light, clean, no fatigue.”
- “Skip prep”
- “Start prep”

Avoid:

- “Rehab protocol”
- “Pain treatment”
- “Correct dysfunction”

### Capacity Focus

Capacity should sound like training, not therapy.

Use:

- “Build tolerance.”
- “Controlled capacity work.”
- “Stop if it causes pain.”
- “This is preparation and capacity work, not medical advice.”

Avoid:

- “Fix your back.”
- “Cure pain.”
- “Therapy.”
- “Rehabilitation.”

### Plan

Plan should sound strategic.

Use:

- “Current phase”
- “Training roadmap”
- “This week”
- “Hypertrophy builds the base.”
- “Strength turns muscle into force.”

Avoid:

- “Template system”
- “Generator logic”
- “Programme metadata”

### Progress

Progress should answer whether training is working.

Use:

- “Training is moving.”
- “Stay the course.”
- “Fatigue is the limiter.”
- “Build more history first.”
- “Recent evidence”

Avoid:

- “Readiness 81”
- “Strategic coaching calculation”
- “MEV/MAV/MRV”
- “Internal signal”

### Library

Library should be searchable, not lecture-heavy.

Use:

- Exercise name.
- Muscle.
- Equipment.
- Movement.

Avoid:

- Difficulty labels by default unless useful.
- Long taxonomy explanations.

### Settings

Settings should feel like preferences.

Use:

- “Units”
- “Available weight jumps”
- “Rep strategy”
- “Capacity focus”
- “Plan management”

Avoid:

- “Drop-off percent” as a primary normal-user concept.
- “Raw sync state” outside dev/staging.
- “Diagnostics” in production.

### Onboarding

Onboarding should feel like a coach asking what matters.

Use:

- “What are we building?”
- “How many days can you actually train?”
- “What equipment do you have?”
- “Pick the plan style.”

Avoid:

- “Configure training model.”
- “Select metadata fields.”

### Paywall

Paywall should sell outcomes without fake promises.

Use:

- “Performance-led training.”
- “Progression earned from your logs.”
- “Clear next steps every session.”

Avoid:

- “Guaranteed muscle.”
- “AI knows best.”
- “Scientifically proven best plan.”

## Forbidden Copy

Do not say:

- Fixes pain.
- Cures injury.
- Rehab.
- Therapy.
- Guaranteed muscle.
- Guaranteed strength.
- Scientifically proven best.
- AI knows best.
- Push through pain.
- Ignore pain.
- Perfect programme.
- Optimal for everyone.

## Preferred Safety Copy

Use:

- “Evidence-informed.”
- “Recommended.”
- “Based on your logged performance.”
- “Stop if pain occurs.”
- “Build capacity.”
- “Earn progression.”
- “Not medical advice.”
- “Seek qualified medical advice for severe, worsening, radiating, numbness, weakness, or concerning symptoms.”

## Recommendation Copy Patterns

### Increase Load

Visible:

“You earned more weight.”

Reason:

“You hit the top of the target range for 3 productive sets.”

Evidence:

- 3 productive work sets.
- Same load.
- No shutdown.

### Hold Load

Visible:

“Hold the weight.”

Reason:

“The work was useful, but the load increase was not earned yet.”

### Reduce Load

Visible:

“Back it down.”

Reason:

“Recent performance says this load is too expensive.”

Evidence:

- Repeated early shutdowns.
- Best-set regression.
- Quality-set collapse.

### Deload

Visible:

“Pull back before pushing again.”

Reason:

“Performance is dropping and fatigue is rising.”

### Rotate Exercise

Visible:

“This lift has stopped paying rent.”

Reason:

“It has stalled across repeated exposures.”

### Low History

Visible:

“Log a few sessions first.”

Reason:

“The app is smart, not psychic.”

## Edgy But Safe

Good:

- “This lift has stopped paying rent.”
- “Bank it.”
- “No junk volume.”
- “The useful work is done.”
- “Hold. Earn cleaner reps.”

Too much:

- “Destroy yourself.”
- “No pain, no gain.”
- “Stop being weak.”
- “Ignore fatigue.”
- “Guaranteed gains.”

## Implementation Recommendations

### Copy Changes I Recommend

1. Replace generic recommendation titles with action-led coach copy.
2. Keep evidence drawers factual, but use clearer evidence headings.
3. Rewrite low-history states to be honest and memorable.
4. Clarify unknown-load percentages with plain-language effort labels.
5. Clarify Power block execution without pretending bar speed is measured.

### Changes To Avoid

1. Do not make every line edgy.
2. Do not sacrifice clarity for personality.
3. Do not use medical claims in Capacity Focus.
4. Do not use “AI” as a trust signal.
5. Do not make the app sound angry at the user.

### Before The Next Build

Copy changes are not mandatory before the next QA build.

If doing a small pass, prioritize:

1. Low-history Progress copy.
2. Hold/reduce/increase recommendation copy.
3. Power block copy.
4. Unknown-load guidance.

### Can Wait

1. Paywall rewrite.
2. Full onboarding rewrite.
3. Advanced evidence drawer tone pass.
