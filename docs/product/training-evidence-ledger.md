# Training evidence ledger

Last updated: 2026-08-23. This ledger records only principles with a plausible product decision attached. It is not a library of programme summaries.

Evidence labels: `strong` = convergent review/position evidence; `moderate` = useful but context-sensitive evidence; `coaching inference` = defensible practice not established as a universal causal rule; `weak/speculative` = must not become authority without testing.

## Progression-volume decision boundary

Current safe behavior:

1. One successful exposure cannot add a set.
2. Three recent exposures can establish an informational volume signal only when progression frequency meets the configured boundary and objective fatigue does not require reduction.
3. An earned load increase takes precedence; volume is not increased simultaneously.
4. Three-exposure exercise stalls hold load and do not automatically increase muscle volume.
5. Two or more fatigue-flagged exercises can recommend a deload; one can recommend volume reduction.

Applicability boundary: this legacy report lacks the full user model, canonical weekly direct/indirect muscle dose, training age, adherence, pain and exercise-fit evidence. Its add-volume result must not become an autonomous prescription authority. The next revision should emit confidence and candidate causes, then defer actual dose mutation to the canonical volume policy.
