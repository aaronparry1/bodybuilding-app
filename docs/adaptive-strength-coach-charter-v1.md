# Adaptive Strength Coach Charter v1

Status: permanent foundational document.

Purpose: this document defines the permanent philosophy of Adaptive Strength Coach.

This Charter sits above implementation, algorithms, user interface, progression systems, Adaptive Stress Allocation, future AI, and all future coaching features.

It should be reviewed only rarely. If another document conflicts with this Charter, this Charter wins.

## Mission

Adaptive Strength Coach exists to help people make meaningful long-term progress through intelligent, evidence-informed coaching.

We are not trying to maximise today's performance.

We are trying to maximise the probability that our athletes continue progressing for years.

## North Star

Adaptive Strength Coach maximises long-term progress by making the smallest coaching intervention that preserves:

- adaptation
- recovery
- confidence
- enjoyment
- momentum

## Our Beliefs

1. Adaptation is the objective. Performance is an outcome.
2. Fatigue is currency. Spend it wisely.
3. Momentum matters. The athlete should feel like they are always moving forward.
4. Confidence matters. Training should build belief, not fear.
5. Recovery is productive. Recovery enables future progress.
6. Progress should feel rewarding. The coach should deliberately create meaningful wins and milestones.
7. Simplicity beats complexity. The athlete should not need to understand the coaching engine.
8. Evidence guides us. Dogma does not.
9. Safety is never sacrificed for short-term gains.
10. The programme adapts to the athlete. The athlete should never have to adapt to the programme.

## Decision Hierarchy

Every coaching decision should follow:

1. Long-term progress
2. Adaptation
3. Recovery
4. Confidence
5. Enjoyment
6. Momentum
7. Performance
8. Variety

Performance is not the objective.

Performance serves progress.

## Coaching Principles

### Minimum Effective Dose

Use the smallest training dose that is likely to move the athlete forward.

More work is not automatically better. More work is only justified when it improves adaptation without creating disproportionate fatigue, complexity, or recovery cost.

### Reduce Overload Faster Than Increase Overload

Adaptive Strength Coach reduces excessive training stress faster than it increases training stress.

The downside of excessive load, fatigue, or joint stress is greater than the downside of being slightly conservative for one session. Added stress must be earned. Excessive stress should be corrected quickly.

### Training Frequency Is A Constraint, Not A Goal

The best training frequency is the one the athlete can recover from, adhere to, and execute well.

Adaptive Strength Coach should not worship frequency. It should distribute useful training around the athlete's real life.

### Never Interpret Successful Progression As Deterioration

If an athlete increases load and stays inside the prescribed target range, that is productive progression.

The app must not punish expected fatigue after a successful heavier-load exposure.

### Every Intervention Has A Cost

Changing load, reps, sets, exercise selection, frequency, effort, or recovery guidance all carries a cost.

The app should intervene only when the expected benefit justifies that cost.

### Prefer The Smallest Effective Intervention

When two decisions are likely to create similar adaptation, choose the one with lower fatigue, lower complexity, and higher adherence.

### Productive Fatigue Is Not Failure

Fatigue is expected when training is effective.

The app should distinguish productive fatigue from deteriorating performance, excessive stress, or poor prescription.

### Recovery Weeks Require Systemic Evidence

A full recovery week should not be triggered by one bad lift, one local failure, or one imperfect session.

Recovery weeks require meaningful systemic evidence, explicit severe recovery feedback, or repeated problems across sessions and movement patterns.

### Compare Performance Within Comparable Workloads

Performance comparisons are only meaningful when the workload context is comparable.

Raw reps at a lighter load should not be directly compared against raw reps at a heavier load as though they represent the same stress.

## Feature Principle

Every future feature must answer:

- Does this improve long-term progress?
- Does this improve coaching quality?
- Does this reduce unnecessary cognitive load?
- Would an excellent coach actually do this?

If not:

Do not build it.

## Scientific Principle

Research informs coaching.

Research does not replace coaching.

The best available evidence, combined with good coaching judgement and real-world athlete outcomes, should guide Adaptive Strength Coach.

## Product Principle

Adaptive Strength Coach should feel like:

The world's most trusted digital strength coach.

Not the world's biggest workout tracker.

## Long-Term Vision

In ten years, users should describe Adaptive Strength Coach with one sentence:

"It always seemed to know exactly what I needed next."

## Ending

This Charter exists to protect the integrity of Adaptive Strength Coach.

Algorithms will evolve.

Science will evolve.

Technology will evolve.

This philosophy should remain.

This document becomes the highest authority within Adaptive Strength Coach.
