Description:
Adaptive Strength Coach is a focused training app for lifters who want structured strength and hypertrophy coaching without spreadsheets, guesswork, or max testing.

Build a plan, log your work, and let the app adjust your training from the evidence you create in the gym.

Adaptive Strength Coach helps you:

- Follow evidence-based strength and hypertrophy workouts
- Train with target zones for each exercise
- Progress with performance-based load and rep recommendations
- Track estimated 1RM trends without testing a true max
- See PRs, recent progress, and advanced reports
- Manage fatigue with Recovery Windows
- Add Recovery & Capacity work when it supports lifting
- Prepare for a Powerlifting Meet with more specific planning

The app does not require RPE or RIR. It watches what you actually complete: load, reps, set quality, history, fatigue signs, consistency, and training context.

Use Adaptive Strength Coach if you want a gym planner, workout tracker, PR tracker, and training plan that behave more like a coach than a spreadsheet.

## App Review-Safe Claims Notes

- Do not promise guaranteed strength gains, fat loss, injury prevention, rehabilitation, or medical outcomes.
- Use "evidence-based" and "performance-based" as product positioning, not as a promise of clinical precision.
- Keep Recovery & Capacity framed as support for training, not medical recovery or calorie burning.
- Keep e1RM framed as estimated strength, not a required max test or guaranteed true 1RM.
- Avoid "AI coach" language unless the product explicitly introduces AI coaching later.
