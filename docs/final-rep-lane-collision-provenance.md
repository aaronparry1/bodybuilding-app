# Final rep/lane collision provenance

D4E3C4D7 carries collision metadata through the final decision without changing production precedence. When role derivation falls through to inference, the decision records `inferred_role` and the existing reason. No rejected authority is invented after the fact.

Override/method/family collision winners and planned-order lane authority remain unavailable in the current helper return contracts. They remain explicit migration blockers. Generated settings, public façades, formulas and downstream outputs are unchanged; production does not consume v2.
