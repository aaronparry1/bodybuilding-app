# Canonical Progress intervention contracts

`canonical_progress_intervention_v1` represents bounded policy intent owned by Progress. It supports load, volume, microcycle rotation, recovery, and goal/phase review families with explicit application-owner metadata and evidence provenance.

Interventions deliberately cannot carry exact loads, sets, reps, exercises, slot volume, session order, or roles. Mesocycle, Microcycle, Session Construction, and Macrocycle remain the owners that validate and apply those consequences.

The executable Design-QA registry contains 78 unique IDs: 13 plan-state, 30 session-lifecycle, and 35 Progress/decision fixtures. Earlier 15/29/34 counts were stale artifacts and are corrected by the executable inventory.
