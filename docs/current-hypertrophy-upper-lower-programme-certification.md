# Hypertrophy Upper/Lower programme certification

D2.5C3 certifies the narrow intermediate four-day Upper/Lower policy structurally. Four policy outputs are adapted with supplied IDs into D2, then hydrated through D1. Session totals are locked at Upper A/B 16–23 and Lower A/B 13–19 direct sets, for 58–84 total direct sets across four sessions.

The certification validates role order, unique supplied identities, movement opportunities and A/B priority shifts. It is not a recoverability, exercise-selection, exact-target or runtime-persistence certification. D2.5D0 classifies it only as a candidate `hypertrophy_base` policy: it must not attach to `hypertrophy_calibration`, which is the present new-plan entry mesocycle.
