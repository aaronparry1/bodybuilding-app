# Final rep/lane authority provenance

D4E3C4D6 enriches the existing final compatibility rep/lane decision with production-native provenance. The decision now records the rep and lane authority source, applied identity, role source, prescription family and ownership stage. Ordinary branches use `block_compatibility`; corrective, recovery and power branches retain their family authority identities.

The enrichment is observational. It does not select a winner, invoke the v2 resolver, alter formulas, or affect generated settings. Public compatibility façades retain their existing signatures and outputs.

Override/method/role collision provenance and planned-order authority are not retained by the current helper contracts. They remain explicit blockers, so caller migration is not certified.
