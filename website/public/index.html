How it coaches

Three things, every session.

Most apps are a notebook with a timer. This one makes the decisions a coach would make, from the evidence you give it.

01 · Tell it

Your goal, your days, your kit.

A full programme is built in a minute: the right split, the right exercises, and a starting load for each one.

02 · Log it

Weight, reps, done.

Two taps a set. The rest timer runs with your phone in your pocket. Swap an exercise if the rack's taken; the plan adapts.

03 · It adjusts

Next session is already set.

Hit the top of the range and the load goes up. Grind and it holds. Fatigue builds and it eases off before you stall. You just turn up.

Progression

Earned, not scheduled.

Linear programmes add weight on a calendar. Adaptive Strength Coach adds weight when you've shown you can lift it, holds when you haven't, and knows the difference between a bad day and a plateau.

  • A target load and rep range for every set
  • Load increases the session after you earn them
  • Plateaus handled by changing the rule, not by pushing harder on the old one

Progress

A verdict, not a graph.

Is training working? The Progress tab answers in a sentence, then shows you why: PRs, estimated 1RM trends with no max testing, and what to do next.

  • Personal records tracked automatically
  • Estimated 1RM for every lift
  • Progress by exercise and muscle group

Recovery

Knows when you're cooked.

Recovery Windows watch for fatigue building across sessions and ease the load before it costs you a week. Optional Recovery & Capacity sessions support the lifting rather than competing with it.

  • Fatigue detected from your logs, not a questionnaire
  • Deloads that happen when they're needed, not on a fixed week
  • Powerlifting meet prep with a proper peak
