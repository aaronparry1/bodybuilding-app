# Evidence-gap register

Register date: 2026-08-08. Scope follows `product-mission-and-scope-decision.md`. Every row is a decision the app may make; interesting topics with no prospective consumer are excluded.

## Reading the register

Timescale codes: `P` programme, `B` block, `W` week, `S` session, `E` exercise, `Set` set, `X` explanation only. Potential strength: `X` explanation, `S` soft heuristic, `M` method definition, `H?` hard-rule candidate pending governance (never an approved hard rule). Status: `O` open, `PC` partly covered, `CPR` covered pending review, `U` unsuitable. Source-request IDs refer to `additional-source-intake-request.md`.

“Held” names evidence already in this audit: `MLR` modern-literature review; `SS` supplied-source candidate/crosswalk; `Repo` repository/test evidence. “Minimum / preferred / useful” describes evidence needed to close the decision gap, not a demand for copyrighted material.

## Register totals and governance

There are **43 decision gaps: 8 critical, 26 high, 9 medium and 0 low**. No row is closed. Existing evidence avoids duplicate intake where it is already adequate for a narrative statement, but no source count alone changes readiness. A gap closes only when its row-specific acceptance criteria, rights/access record, page/section traceability, limitations/conflicts and scientific/product review are complete. Medical or safety hard-rule candidates require the appropriate qualified owner; all others default to no change or a bounded soft policy.
