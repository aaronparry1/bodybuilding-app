## Ten evidence bundles to source first

1. **Q1-01 — hypertrophy dose:** recent trained-adult volume dose-response synthesis plus key trials, including per-muscle weekly and per-session dose.
2. **Q1-03 — proximity/failure/RIR:** trained-participant failure comparisons plus exercise- and experience-specific RIR-validity studies; do not duplicate the broad reviews already held.
3. **Q1-06 — regional hypertrophy and ROM:** synthesis plus longitudinal imaging trials covering exercise specificity, long-muscle-length work and partial/full ROM.
4. **Q1-04 — load range and set termination:** effort/volume-controlled load-range evidence plus rep-/velocity-loss dose-response and device validity.
5. **Q1-09 — strength specificity/frequency:** powerlifting-relevant synthesis and trained trials reporting lift practice, heavy exposures, volume and intensity.
6. **Q1-13 — exercise-specific e1RM:** validation across squat, bench, deadlift, machines/isolation exercises and rep ranges with error tables.
7. **Q1-14 — observation confidence:** repeated within-person research on daily variability, smallest detectable change, evidence accumulation/decay and false responder classification.
8. **Q1-19 — population modifiers:** recent sex-, age- and training-status-stratified synthesis/meta-regression plus underrepresented key trials.
9. **Q1-10 — strength progression/periodisation:** current synthesis plus advanced-lifter cohorts comparing linear, DUP and block structures and documenting plateau logic.
10. **Q1-18 — powerbuilding phase transitions:** longitudinal evidence measuring both hypertrophy and strength across sequencing, maintenance and transition strategies.

## Do not obtain duplicates for these established narrative positions

Existing evidence is sufficient to retain the following **narrative boundaries**, though it is not sufficient to implement every associated policy:

- Hypertrophy frequency is primarily a way to distribute volume when volume is equated.
- Momentary failure is not universally required; obtain only evidence that sharpens exercise/population/RIR decisions.
- Adaptation occurs across broad load ranges; do not source another generic repetition-zone chart.
- Advanced methods have no established blanket superiority; obtain method-specific purpose/dose/stop evidence only.
- A mandatory fourth-week deload is not a scientific invariant.
- Manuals/coaching systems define methods and history but do not scientifically validate them.
- Sport-specific programming is outside this product mission.
- Energy deficit is a recovery/retention context, not a fat-loss or nutrition lane.
- Pain/injury remains a non-diagnostic stop/referral boundary.
- Source 16 is unresolved, low priority and non-blocking; Aaron does not need to source it for this roadmap.
