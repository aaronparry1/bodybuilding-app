# Supplied-source copyright and usage cautions

## Boundary

Possession of the 16 PDFs does not establish ownership, redistribution permission, a software/content licence, public-domain status or permission to train a model on them. Several are recognisable commercial books, courses, manuals, article compilations or scans; some have uncertain provenance. This audit is not legal advice.

Allowed audit behaviour was deliberately narrow: inspect locally, record bibliographic/provenance uncertainty, paraphrase short claim candidates, cite PDF page locations, compare ideas with modern evidence, and avoid committing the PDFs or page images. No report reproduces a routine, chapter, table, illustration or substantial passage.

## Product cautions

- Do not ship the PDFs, scans, OCR text, covers, tables, exercise illustrations or substantial excerpts without a documented licence.
- Do not recreate named commercial programmes or full routine sequences merely by changing formatting. Method ideas may be unprotectable in some jurisdictions, but expression, selection/arrangement, branding and trademarks can still create risk.
- Treat names such as programme/product titles as attribution, not as endorsement or licensed branding. Obtain legal review before using them in customer-facing template names.
- Use original neutral descriptions for generic methods such as rest-pause, cluster sets, pyramids, accommodating resistance or autoregulation, and cite contemporary evidence for outcome claims.
- Do not use the corpus for model training, retrieval-augmented generation, bulk ingestion or user-visible quotation unless rights and privacy/provenance are documented for that exact use.
- Historical material can contain obsolete safety, medical, nutrition, body-image or gender assumptions. It must never bypass current safety policy or qualified medical guidance.
- Source 9 is a compilation/database and sources 2 and 13 are compilations with uncertain upstream permissions; downstream reuse is especially risky.
- Source 16 is corrupt. An internet-hosted similarly titled copy must not be substituted without identity and rights checks.

## Required rights record

Before content reuse, record: exact file hash and edition; author/publisher; acquisition source; licence/permission and territory; permitted media, excerpt length and modification rights; trademark/attribution conditions; model-training/RAG permission if relevant; expiry/revocation terms; and the accountable legal/content owner. Absence of a rights record means **research reference only**.
