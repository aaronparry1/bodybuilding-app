## Authoritative active order after owner scope decision

The following sequence supersedes any implied ordering above while preserving the original findings:

1. Candidate 111 retained-device resolution.
2. Physical Android and iOS critical-journey certification.
3. Persistence, account isolation, backup, restore and remote-readback truth.
4. Native performance baselines and budgets.
5. Cross-platform workout reliability and execution speed.
6. Coaching-authority consolidation and athlete-model development.
7. Evidence-registry implementation, beginning with the 43 decision gaps and without executable promotion.
8. Programme-quality expansion exclusively for hypertrophy, strength and powerbuilding.
9. Premium cross-platform UI and explanation improvements.
10. Controlled advanced-method expansion only after purpose, eligibility, dosage/progression, stopping and exit are specified.
11. Experiments and optimisation with preregistered safe metrics.

Sport-specific S&C, conditioning, endurance/concurrent-sport, strongman-sport, Olympic-weightlifting performance and generic athletic development have no active milestone. A separate future product may reuse non-coaching infrastructure. Energy deficit is only a recovery/expectation modifier within the three active lanes. Evidence requests are attached to stages 6–10 in `evidence-gap-register.md`; they do not move those stages ahead of reliability.
