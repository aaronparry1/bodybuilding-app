# Supplied-source modern-evidence crosswalk

## Outcome

The corpus is useful for method vocabulary, programme architecture and historical context. It does not supply modern comparative certainty. The crosswalk below therefore tests themes rather than treating authorial prescriptions as proof. Modern reviews are the higher evidence layer; practical manuals can still define the method being discussed.

| Theme and candidates | Contemporary evidence | Reconciliation | Product consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Failure/proximity: SSC-02, SSC-18, SSC-21, SSC-23 | Meta-analyses find no clear requirement to train to momentary failure for strength or hypertrophy; newer dose-response work suggests proximity matters, especially for hypertrophy, without validating failure on every set. [Grgic et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33497853/), [Refalo et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36334240/), [Robinson et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38970765/) | Wendler's stop-before-failure cue is compatible as a method. Universal all-set failure and rigid “threshold” claims are not. | Store target RIR/range, actual RIR confidence and stop reasons; never mandate failure globally. |
| Frequency and split choice: corpus programme templates | When volume is equated, higher frequency has no clear meaningful hypertrophy advantage; frequency is largely a way to distribute tolerable weekly work. [Schoenfeld et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30558493/) | Fixed two-, three- or four-day layouts are templates, not biologically privileged schedules. | Choose split from availability, exposure needs, recovery and preference; allow reflow without erasing intent. |
| Periodisation, waves and deloads: SSC-04, SSC-08, SSC-16, SSC-26 | Periodised training tends to improve 1RM strength over non-periodised training, particularly in trained participants, but evidence does not establish one universal model or a mandatory fourth-week deload; hypertrophy differences are small/uncertain when volume is equated. [Williams et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28497285/), [Moesgaard et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35044672/) | Wave/tier/conjugate schemes are reasonable architectures. Exact rotation and deload calendars remain configurable heuristics. | Represent phase, emphasis, priority and deload rationale explicitly; adaptation must be evidence/log driven. |
| Rep/load zones: SSC-01, SSC-14, SSC-19, SSC-24 | The supplied corpus' sharp rep boundaries are not supported as exclusive adaptation zones. Outcomes depend on load, effort, volume, exercise and goal. | Training-max conservatism and density schemes can be bounded operational choices; “1–5 strength only, 6–15 mass” is historical simplification. | Present ranges as overlapping goal-biased options with fatigue and skill constraints, not deterministic labels. |
| Rest and density: SSC-05, SSC-06, SSC-20, SSC-24 | A recent Bayesian meta-analysis suggests longer inter-set rests can modestly favour hypertrophy, while method-specific rest must also protect output/technique. [Singer et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39205815/) | Short-rest density work is a method, not universally superior. Full-rest sprint quality and longer heavy-set rest are task-dependent. | Rest is a first-class prescription and observation; warn when density compromises target output. |
| Advanced methods: SSC-05, SSC-15, SSC-20 | Reviews of drop sets, clusters, supersets and advanced methods generally support time-efficiency or comparable adaptations in context, not broad superiority. [Drop-set review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37523092/), [cluster-set review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32901442/), [advanced-method review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38654909/), [superset review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39903375/) | Manual-derived methods belong in an optional method library with clear cost/benefit, not as “secret” optimisers. | Gate by experience, exercise safety and time goal; capture method-specific completion/fatigue. |
| Autoregulation and velocity: SSC-01, SSC-09, SSC-10, SSC-17 | Modern reviews support autoregulation/VBT as useful approaches in appropriate settings, but device quality and implementation matter. [VBT review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35380511/), [autoregulation review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40791980/) | Conservative training maxes and quality-first adjustment are compatible; they do not justify opaque automatic changes. | Every change needs signal, confidence, guardrail and explanation; manual override remains authoritative. |
| Concurrent endurance/strength: SSC-23 | Classic and newer reviews find interference is moderated by outcome, modality, dose, scheduling and population rather than being inevitable. [Wilson et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22002517/), [updated systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933151/) | Mentzer's blanket claim is rejected. Later mission decision makes endurance programming out of scope. | Retain as historical/contextual evidence only; declared outside activity may contextualise recovery but the app does not prescribe it. |
| Specificity/dynamic correspondence: SSC-07, SSC-11, SSC-12 | These are useful organizing concepts, but resemblance alone does not prove transfer. | Retain as an explainable demand-matching framework, with outcomes verified in the target sport/task. | Store the dimension being matched—force direction, ROM, velocity, skill or energy demand—and mark uncertainty. |

## Evidence decision

The supplied corpus changes the audit from “source pack absent” to “source pack appraised.” It does **not** increase the count of scientifically validated hard rules. The safe near-term value is a richer controlled vocabulary, source-attributable explanations and candidate templates. All quantitative prescriptions remain claims requiring modern appraisal, versioning, monitoring and product-policy approval.

This conclusion covers the 15 inspected historical sources. Source 16 remains unreviewed and contributes no claim or modern crosswalk. Its strongman-sport focus is outside the active mission; any future accessory principle would still need independent current evidence mapped to an existing hypertrophy, strength or powerbuilding gap.
