# Evidence hierarchy and policy conversion

Priority: systematic reviews/meta-analyses; consensus/position stands; controlled trials; longitudinal observations; foundational texts; expert systems; anecdote/history. Recency does not automatically outrank quality, and commercial manuals never become scientific authority by repetition.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Evidence["Retrieved evidence"] --> Claim["Reviewed claim"]
  Claim --> Policy["Approved coaching policy"]
  Policy --> Rule["Versioned deterministic rule"]
  Rule --> Decision["Athlete-specific decision"]
  Decision --> Explain["User explanation + evidence IDs"]
```

Hard rules are reserved for safety, identity, data integrity or very high-confidence invariants. Soft heuristics require bounds and fallbacks. Explanation-only evidence may educate but cannot mutate prescriptions. Literature never directly generates workouts; AI may retrieve/summarize/explain/gap-detect but cannot override protected deterministic authorities.

## Supplied-source placement

The inspected manuals, textbooks, courses, slides, thesis and compilations sit at the foundational-text/expert-system/history layers. They may define a method, expose a programming variable or supply a hypothesis, but they cannot vote themselves into policy through repetition. Of 26 candidates from 15 inspected sources, none qualifies as a hard product rule; two are rejected, one is insufficient, and the remainder are definitions, explanations, historical context or soft heuristics pending registry review. Unresolved Source 16 contributes no claim and is not a gate.

Conversion must retain both citations: the exact supplied-source page establishes provenance for the historical/manual statement, while current reviewed evidence establishes (or fails to establish) applicability. Conflict defaults to the narrower, safer interpretation and records uncertainty rather than averaging sources.

## Mission and intake gate

Evidence relevance now requires a prospective decision in hypertrophy, strength, powerbuilding, their safety/population context or product adherence. Sport-specific material cannot enter current policy merely because a principle might transfer; the abstracted claim must map to a registered decision and receive independent contemporary support. Energy-deficit material can modify training context but cannot create nutrition or fat-loss policy. Acquisition priority follows `additional-source-intake-request.md`, not source quantity or author prestige.
