## Product conversion gate

Before any candidate becomes executable it needs: a precise population/outcome statement; contemporary evidence grade; contraindications and medical boundary; load/volume/fatigue guardrails; explainable user-facing wording; claim-registry ownership and review date; and tests showing that UI, generator, storage and analytics use the same semantics. Named routines and proprietary prose must not be copied merely because a method is technically implementable.
