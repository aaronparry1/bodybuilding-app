## Intake directory and naming

Place future material under `project_sources/evidence_intake/` using:

```text
01_hypertrophy_volume/
02_effort_failure_and_fatigue/
03_exercise_selection_and_biomechanics/
04_strength_programming/
05_autoregulation_and_athlete_modelling/
06_advanced_methods/
07_programme_construction/
08_population_and_context/
09_adherence_and_product_ux/
10_coaching_systems_and_competitors/
99_unclassified/
```

The directories are documented but not created: empty directories would not be represented in this documentation-only commit, and Source 16 must remain untouched. Preferred filename:

`GAP-ID__FirstAuthor-Or-Organisation__Year__Short-Title.pdf`

Examples: `HYP-VOL-01__Baz-Valle__2022__Volume-Hypertrophy-Review.pdf`, `AUTO-E1RM-01__FirstAuthor__2024__Exercise-Specific-e1RM-Validity.pdf`, `METHOD-RP-01__FirstAuthor__2023__Rest-Pause-Trained-Lifters.pdf`. For books/manuals use `GAP-ID__Author__Year-or-Unknown__Book-or-Manual-Title.pdf`.

If convenient, add `GAP-ID__source-note.md` with full citation; DOI/PMID/ISBN/stable URL; why it seems relevant; whether it is a complete legal copy; useful pages/chapters; and scan/OCR problems. The note is optional. Unlabelled lawful material is accepted in `99_unclassified/` for later classification.
