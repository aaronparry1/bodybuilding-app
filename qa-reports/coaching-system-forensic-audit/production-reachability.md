## Production entrypoint proof

`app.config.ts` resolves the production router root to `app-production`. Production protected routes re-export the shared repaired screens. Mounted Home, Plan, Train, Progress, History, onboarding and Extra Session use canonical carrier/ledger/projection boundaries.

## Authoritative and reachable

- Initial Macrocycle and Mesocycle.
- Microcycle schedule/framework.
- Session Construction exact prescriptions.
- Initial volume allocation.
- Extra sessions.
- Supported advanced methods.
- Conservative PR/progress presentation.

## Incomplete

- Macrocycle pathway beyond the first phase.
- Load calibration becoming an established future load.
- Later recovery/fatigue response.
- Taper/peak progression toward an event.
- Later coaching explanations.

## Unreachable or contradicted

- Completed-performance-driven Mesocycle transition.
- Missed-session-driven Microcycle reflow.
- Future volume adaptation.
- Automatic exact load progression/regression.
- Evidence-led deload.
- Mounted exercise substitution/learned preference.

## Registered disconnected surfaces

The protected production layout registers:

- `programmes/builder`;
- `programmes/session`;
- `session-prep`.

The session builder writes `programmeRepository`, selects a legacy custom day and presents “Start Session.” It does not establish canonical plan/session identity and cannot overwrite the canonical carrier. These routes are therefore not a second active authority, but they are a competing product surface and should be isolated after caller/deep-link review.
