# Dependency-ordered Phase 2 sequence

No Phase 2 production behavior was implemented in this certification.

## 1. Transaction and truthful-application closure

Repair deterministic completion-evidence reconciliation and require an enumerated compatible future target delta before recording `applied`. Certify CAS, crash points, receipt reconstruction, and duplicate concurrency independently.

## 2. Safe continuity at review and Mesocycle boundaries

Define a machine-evaluable, source-backed objective/continuation contract. Ensure a blocked transition cannot consume the last session without a resolvable next action. Do not infer transition merely from elapsed weeks.

## 3. Comparable-exposure identity

Version a comparator using exercise identity plus loading mode, prescription family, role where material, freshness, substitution status, and cycle context. Migrate old evidence fail-closed without inventing observations.

## 4. Mounted factual coaching inputs

Persist athlete-authored or sensor-independent readiness, pain/limitation, capacity, missed-session, layoff, equipment, and sport-workload facts with corrections, timestamps, freshness ownership, and restart replay. First expose review-only outcomes.

## 5. Bounded progression and plateau policy

Add source-backed numeric or rep-range escalation separately for eligible exercises/equipment. Distinguish normal and high responders; qualify repeated stalls using the new comparator. Session Construction remains exact-prescription owner.

## 6. Recovery, deload, and transition operation

Compose factual recovery with Mesocycle fatigue/deload and approved successor policy. Add atomic applications only after the full decision chain, history preservation, and stale/conflict tests exist.

## 7. Missed sessions, layoff, substitution, and limitation behavior

Implement independently certifiable reflow/recalibration rules. Substitutions must never inherit incompatible loads. Equipment and limitations must survive restart and future construction.

## 8. Volume, density, and contextual method retention/removal

Only after evidence and progression foundations are mounted, add source-backed bounded volume/density adaptation and method retention/removal. Do not add methods for variety.

## 9. Athlete-facing explanations

Project the factual evidence, decision, exact committed delta, blocked reason, and resolution route. UI remains projection-only.

Each step is a separate gate with production-path, restart, counterfactual, longitudinal, and fault-injection certification.
