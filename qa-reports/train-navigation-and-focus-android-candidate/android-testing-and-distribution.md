# Android testing and distribution

The completed artifact is a production-signed Android App Bundle intended for Google Play. An AAB is uploaded to Play Console; it is not the direct sideload format used like an APK.

No Google Play upload or release occurred in this task. The next separately authorised action is to upload this exact AAB to the existing Play Console application and assign it to an internal testing release. That action changes external store state and should be requested explicitly.

Physical Android verification remains unproven until the Play internal-testing build is installed and exercised on a genuine Android device.
