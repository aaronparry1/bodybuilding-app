# Genuine iPhone verification checklist

1. Fresh launch and authenticated Home state.
2. Confirm Home, Plan and Train identify the same next workout.
3. Open the planned workout preview.
4. Confirm dark/gold branding on preview, cards, text and controls.
5. Start the workout.
6. Inspect exercise cards, set rows, prescribed targets and Complete Set controls.
7. Complete one set and inspect completed styling.
8. Start, pause, resume, extend and skip the rest timer.
9. Close and reopen the app; confirm the active workout resumes correctly.
10. Open Discard and cancel it.
11. Reopen Discard and confirm it.
12. Confirm navigation returns to the expected destination.
13. Confirm there is no ghost active workout.
14. Confirm the planned workout is restored exactly.
15. Confirm Home, Plan and Train remain consistent.
16. Restart the app and confirm the discarded attempt does not reopen.
17. Start the restored workout again.
18. Complete a separate workout normally and inspect the summary.
19. Record screenshots and exact reproduction steps for any visual or lifecycle defect.
20. Record programming-quality concerns separately from this repair.

Uploading a binary does not complete this checklist.
