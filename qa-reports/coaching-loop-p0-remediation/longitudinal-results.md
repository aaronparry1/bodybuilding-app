All scenarios execute through the mounted completion path, persist evidence/evaluation/decision/application state, hydrate after persistence, and repeat the identical completion idempotently.

Five scenarios produce an appropriate bounded future change. Seven produce an explicit justified no-change or blocked decision. None silently omits adaptation.

The result does **not** certify automatic numeric load progression, numeric regression, or automatic deload. Those remain fail-closed pending approved canonical authority.
