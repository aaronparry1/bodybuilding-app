# Evidence-governed coaching-quality backlog

The owner reports sessions around 15 sets, excessive straight sets, weak progression stimulus and too few purposeful supersets/advanced structures. These concerns are important but are not release-recovery changes. The current documentation mission and evidence register remain authoritative.

| Finding | Classification now | Evidence needed before change | Future consumer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low or poorly distributed session/weekly volume | possible policy/output-quality defect; not yet isolated to code | capture generated plan inputs, per-muscle direct/indirect sets, weekly distribution and applicable `HYP-VOL-*` decisions | volume allocator and programme constructor |
| Approximately 15 sets per session | observation, not intrinsically pass/fail | compare duration, muscle/lift exposure, weekly dose, recoverability and omitted priorities | session feasibility and split policy |
| Predominantly straight sets | current mounted default/limited breadth, not automatically a malfunction | demonstrate intended method policy selected a non-straight method but output lost it, otherwise treat as `METHOD-EFF-01`/`METHOD-SET-02` enhancement | controlled method library |
| Inadequate progression stimulus | potential implementation bug only if stored prescription/rule/observations demand a change that was not applied | exact rule version, comparable observations, decision receipt, resulting prescription and `EFF-PROG-05`/strength policy | adaptation engine |
| Missing purposeful supersets | future enhancement unless an existing certified superset fixture fails reachability | purpose, eligibility, pairing, dosage, fatigue, stop/exit and method-specific evidence | method library/session constructor |
| Other advanced methods | explicitly deferred | each method must satisfy purpose, eligibility, dosage, progression, stop, exit, fallback, rights and golden-test contract | post-registry controlled expansion |

## Required diagnostic sample

For a real quality investigation, capture a redacted deterministic fixture: goal lane, experience, days, duration, equipment, split, plan/revision, phase, session roles, exercise IDs/roles, sets/reps/load state, method IDs, progression policies and decision receipts. Do not use a screenshot or total-set count alone to rewrite programme policy.

No advanced method, volume target or progression rule is approved by this backlog. Reliability priorities, athlete-model governance and the evidence registry remain prerequisites.
