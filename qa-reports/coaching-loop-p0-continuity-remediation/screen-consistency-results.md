# Home, Plan, and Train consistency

All three surfaces remain projections of one committed canonical active-plan
carrier.

- A regenerated same-phase session has one shared ID in Home action, Plan next
  actionable session, and Train prescription lookup.
- An approved successor is constructed and CAS-committed before any surface
  sees its next session.
- If that successor cannot construct, every surface sees either the
  CAS-committed same-phase continuation within the certified horizon or the
  same persisted blocked state at the maximum horizon; no surface presents the
  requested transition as completed.
- Limited equipment and recorded limitations remain in the carrier after
  transition.
- A no-op receipt does not change the carrier revision or future session IDs;
  its read-model explanation comes from the truthful v2 receipt.
- A failed application leaves every surface on the prior carrier.
- A maximum-horizon review with no approved successor has no fabricated next
  session and exposes the persisted review reason through the shared Progress
  decision projection.

Proof:

- `Home, Plan and Train resolve the same regenerated future-session identity`
- `constructs an approved successor atomically while retaining limited
  equipment and limitations`
- `continues the current Mesocycle within its certified horizon when an
  approved successor cannot be constructed`
- `does not claim an applied coaching change when reconstruction is materially
  equivalent`
- existing Home/Plan/Train projection and production-boundary suites.
