## Truthful application

Across 648 completed-session decisions:

- 117 advanced a Microcycle with a material prescription delta;
- 22 applied a constructible approved Mesocycle transition;
- 12 applied a material first-calibration change;
- 1 applied a material recalibration change;
- 5 continued the current Mesocycle within its existing maximum horizon when
  an approved successor could not construct;
- 27 requested changes resolved to
  `material_prescription_delta_absent` and did not write a plan revision;
- 464 maintained the material future prescription explicitly.

Every `applied` receipt advanced the plan revision and contained exact
before/after deltas. Every `unchanged` receipt retained its revision and
contained no deltas.

## Boundary result

The prior `machine_evaluable_objective_policy_missing` deadlock is absent.
Ordinary, partial, and repeated-failure boundaries continue through existing
Mesocycle horizons. Approved successors are used only when Session Construction
can produce a compatible plan. A failed successor construction remains
truthful: the current phase continues only while its certified maximum horizon
permits it.

## Evidence limits

For continuity claims the runner deliberately excludes directly injected
recovery, returning-capacity, pain, and changing-sport-workload evidence because
production has no mounted writer for those facts. Those scenario names are
retained to preserve the certified matrix, but only mounted performed-work,
completion, carrier constraints, and cycle facts drive these runs.

The evidence therefore proves bounded longitudinal continuity and truthful
application. It does not prove the Phase 2 recovery, pain, missed-session,
numeric progression, deload, or richer comparable-history capabilities.
