# Remaining Phase 2 findings

This remediation deliberately closes only RB-P0-01, RB-P0-02, and RB-P0-03.
It does not claim broader coaching intelligence.

## P1

1. No mounted factual recovery/readiness/pain/capacity writer.
2. Comparable-history policy still needs a versioned prescription-context
   comparator beyond exercise identity.
3. Normal and high responders do not yet receive source-backed automatic
   numeric progression.
4. Missed planned sessions have no mounted factual evidence/reflow policy.
5. Mounted onboarding equipment coverage remains incomplete.
6. Substitution/removal/reintroduction has no longitudinal adaptation policy.
7. Factual deload eligibility and automatic transition beyond existing approved
   cycle continuity remain unsupported.

## P2

- Freshness is still supplied by evidence contracts rather than a complete
  mounted collection lifecycle.
- Partial-completion semantics are conservative and do not yet distinguish all
  authorised missed-work causes.
- Some policy capabilities remain inactive because production does not collect
  their required facts.
- Athlete-facing explanations expose truthful result/reason but do not yet offer
  a full evidence-detail experience.

## P3

- Diagnostic/reporting terminology can be made more athlete-facing later.
- Broader method repertoire remains a separate evidence-led programming task.

The next safe sequence is: comparable-exposure identity; mounted factual
inputs; independently certified progression/plateau policy; recovery/deload
operation; missed-session/substitution/layoff handling; then optional
volume/density/method refinement and richer explanations.
