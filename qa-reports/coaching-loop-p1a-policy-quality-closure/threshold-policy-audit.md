# Threshold policy audit

Overall verdict: **PARTIALLY PROVEN**.

## Current rule

- Fewer than three comparable completed exposures: insufficient evidence.
- Three latest successful comparable exposures: progress one exact repetition, or one available load increment at the repetition ceiling.
- One failed exposure: hold.
- Two latest failed comparable exposures with at least three total exposures: regress one exact repetition, or one increment at the repetition floor.
- Every prescribed working set must exist once, be complete and meet its exact target at the immutable prescribed load for an exposure to count as success.

## Policy-quality verdicts

| Rule | Classification | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Repetition or load can supply progressive overload | direct experimental support | **PROVEN** |
| Requiring three successful exposures | explicit conservative product-policy decision | **PARTIALLY PROVEN** |
| Requiring two consecutive failed exposures | explicit conservative product-policy decision | **PARTIALLY PROVEN** |
| One anomalous failure holds | reasonable engineering interpretation | **PROVEN** |
| Exact target completion and material overachievement receive the same success value | insufficiently supported simplification | **NOT PROVEN** |
| All sets, rather than the best set, determine success | reasonable integrity rule | **PROVEN** |
| Same exposure count at once-weekly and higher frequency | exposure-based product decision; frequency is not a multiplier | **PARTIALLY PROVEN** |
| Same threshold for primary, secondary and isolation roles | insufficiently differentiated | **NOT PROVEN** |

## Mounted phase coverage

The 22 Mesocycle policies resolve to 11 standard, five deload, four speed/power, one peak and one taper state. Automatic P1A numeric change is available only in standard straight-set prescriptions. Deload, taper, peak, realisation and speed/power contexts fail closed. Getting Lean uses the Hypertrophy Macrocycle engine and therefore has no separate numeric threshold.

- Hypertrophy: standard calibration/base/volume/specialisation paths can use the rule; consolidation/transition prohibit it.
- Powerbuilding: foundation/hypertrophy/strength can use it; intensification, realisation and transition prohibit it.
- Strength: general and accumulation can use it; specific, intensification, taper and transition prohibit it.
- Athletic Performance: general and force can use it; power and transition prohibit it.

No threshold change is justified in this audit. The narrow next policy study should compare the present conservative rule with a role-aware rule using opportunity-level production facts, especially whether repeated exact completion versus repeatable over-target performance should produce different decisions.
