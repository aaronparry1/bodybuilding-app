## Current generic adaptation trace

1. Session construction persists `method`, `methodStructure`, the v2 method contract, exact targets and load prescription in the immutable slot.
2. Recording and ledger reconciliation copy method name, execution kind, policy ID and a semantic method fingerprint into `canonical_progress_evidence_v1` performance observations.
3. Correction projection supersedes the original performed event before evidence reconciliation. Substitution retains its explicit substitution ID and separate exercise identity.
4. `deriveCanonicalNumericPrescriptionDecisions` groups evidence by a per-exercise comparable-exposure key.
5. The same function explicitly returns `phase_prohibited:numeric_change_requires_comparable_straight_sets` for every non-straight method, including antagonist supersets, top-set/back-off work and rest-pause.
6. The generic Progress evaluator can therefore observe method-labelled evidence but cannot classify method execution, suitability, interference, set-role outcomes or rest compliance.
7. `CanonicalProgressDecision` carries only the generic numeric decisions. Its actionable outcome vocabulary is limited to load/repetition progress/regression.
8. `applyPhaseOneDecision` can apply exact load/repetition deltas through durable intent, CAS and receipt handling. It has no method mutation vocabulary and drops unmatched numeric intents at a Mesocycle transition.
9. Application receipts and `appliedDecisionSources` describe only the four numeric outcomes. Reconstruction can restore that durable intent but cannot restore a method-specific decision that was never persisted.
10. Completion and Progress explanations are consequently generic exercise load/repetition explanations rather than persisted method-outcome explanations.
