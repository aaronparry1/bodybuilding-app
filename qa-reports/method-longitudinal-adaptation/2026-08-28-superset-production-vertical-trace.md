## Recovery timing contract introduced in this slice

- A-to-B transition from B-to-next-round-A recovery.
- Continuous foreground elapsed time.
- User-paused duration.
- Background duration.
- Manual timer extension.
- Manual skip.
- Out-of-order execution.
