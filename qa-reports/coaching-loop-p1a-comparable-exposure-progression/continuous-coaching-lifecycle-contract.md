# Continuous coaching lifecycle

Twelve weeks is not encoded as a terminal condition. Each completed Microcycle resolves through current-phase continuation, an approved Mesocycle successor, or a typed review/blocked state. Completed history remains immutable.

The current production graph can cycle through approved Mesocycle successors within one Macrocycle. It does not yet prove construction of a new successor Macrocycle from retained athlete evidence. Continuous coaching is therefore **PARTIALLY PROVEN**, while successor-Macrocycle construction is **NOT PROVEN**.

A future Macrocycle transition must retain canonical exercise history, comparable evidence, established loads, decisions, current limitations/preferences/equipment, and immutable history, while rejecting expired event demands, stale facts and phase-specific prescriptions.
