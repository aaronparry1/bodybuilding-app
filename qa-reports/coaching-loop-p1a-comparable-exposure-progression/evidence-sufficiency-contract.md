# Evidence sufficiency

- First exposure: `insufficient_evidence`.
- Two comparable completed exposures: `insufficient_evidence`.
- Three comparable completed exposures: established trend window.
- Three successive successful comparable exposures: bounded progression eligibility.
- One underperformance: hold.
- Two successive comparable underperformances with at least three total comparable exposures: bounded regression eligibility.
- Recovery after regression: must accumulate a new three-success recent window.
- Calibration-required load: calibration, not progression.
- Substitution, equipment/loading change, method change, missing semantic fields, or different Mesocycle: incomparable/fail closed.
- Deload, taper, peak, transition, grouped methods, unsupported rules, bodyweight-only state, invalid increments, partial/discarded/duplicate evidence: prohibited, held, or factual review.

The rule is based on completed comparable exposures, not a single anomalous set. Recovery/pain/limitation evidence is used only when production actually persists it; absence is never treated as good recovery.
