# Draft support messages — approval required

## Missing programme or history
Please don’t uninstall the app, clear storage, repeat setup or create a replacement programme. Your data may still be on this device. Update through Google Play when we provide the recovery version, then send the redacted Recovery diagnostic summary privately to support.

## Startup crash
Please keep the app installed and don’t clear its storage. Tell support the installed version shown by Android/Google Play and, if the app opens, share only its redacted Recovery diagnostic summary.

## Backup pending or failed
Your local training data remains on this device. Stay signed into the intended account and use Retry backup. Do not log out or reinstall. “Verified” appears only after the complete backup is read back from your account.

## Update from 109
Open the app’s Google Play page and choose Update. Do not uninstall first. After updating, open the app, confirm your programme and history, then force-close and reopen twice.

## Identity mismatch or SQLite failure
Stop programme changes and do not log out, clear storage or reinstall. Send the redacted diagnostic summary privately to support so we can preserve and recover the correct local store.
