# Affected-user recovery

1. Do not uninstall, clear storage, log out, repeat onboarding, or regenerate a programme.
2. Keep the current installation and update only through Google Play when the recovery candidate is assigned.
3. Open Settings → Recovery diagnostic summary and share only the generated redacted/count-only text privately with support.
4. If SQLite or identity mismatch is reported, stop changing the programme and contact support.
5. After recovery, confirm programme/history counts before and after two restarts.
6. Retry backup while signed into the intended account; “verified” is valid only after remote readback and an empty queue.

Never share tokens, database files, raw health/training records or passwords.
