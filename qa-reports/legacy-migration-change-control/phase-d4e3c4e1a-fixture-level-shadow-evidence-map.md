# D4E3C4E1A fixture-level shadow evidence map

The prior shadow implementation recorded only aggregate categories. This phase records the independent D4E3C4D1 fixture inventory explicitly and preserves unavailable production identities instead of inferring them from values. The three ordinary fixtures have primitive value equivalence, but their complete final branch, v2 authority translation, and rollback-output evidence are not present in the current shadow path; they therefore remain ineligible.

The evidence contract is read-only and diagnostic. It is not passed to generated settings, persisted, serialized, or used by runtime control flow. D4E3C4E2A certification remains unchanged and consumes this evidence conservatively.
