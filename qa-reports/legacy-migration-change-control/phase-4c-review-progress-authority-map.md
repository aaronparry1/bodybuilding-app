## Required boundary

For a planned completed workout with a complete `prescribedSetTargets` array, set ordinal `n` is evaluated only against `prescribedSetTargets[n]`. A later range, block, template, mesocycle, or plan change cannot alter the resulting historical progression qualification. Records without stored exact targets retain the explicit compatibility branch.
