## Certified policy audit

The D2.5C3 policy has four ordered templates, direct guidance totals of Upper A/B 16–23 and Lower A/B 13–19 (58–84 total), intermediate/full-gym assumptions, direct accessory opportunities and normal productive priority. Those characteristics fit the catalogue’s `hypertrophy_base` description better than the conservative baseline-establishing calibration state or local-volume-expansion state. This is evidence for a candidate owner only; no code mapping is approved.

## Mapping boundary

Input facts: exact goal, experience, frequency, split, exact `MesocycleId`, equipment and registry version. A mapped response names a policy/certification/version and scope. It cannot use prefix matching, catalogue order, successor state, block data or generic goal; it emits no slots and owns no programme creation.

## D3 implications

Normal new build-muscle plans start in `hypertrophy_calibration`, so they remain compatibility-only. A base-start fixture could reach a future restricted D3 only after D2.5D2. Production D3 needs a dedicated calibration policy/certification or a separately approved change to entry mesocycle; no partial/empty programme container is permitted.
